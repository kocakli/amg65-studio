Front-facing, almost orthographic, very subtle depth and soft upper-left light. Elegant, restrained and crisp, readable at 16px and 32px; generous negative space, strong silhouette. Match a dark graphite and mint desktop app. No text, no letters, no numbers, no provider logos, no watermark, no surrounding scene, no mockup, no additional icons. Output a single finished app icon, preserving alpha outside the tile.
